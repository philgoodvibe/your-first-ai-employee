# Mission 2 - AI Employee Roadmap

> **For [EMPLOYEE NAME]:** Mission 1 should be complete and reviewed before starting. Reference `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `context-folder/`, and `01-social-seo-strategy/`.

## The Mission

Create an AI Employee Roadmap for [BUSINESS NAME].

The roadmap should recommend which AI employees the business should hire next, what each employee should do, what tools or data each would need, how they should coordinate, and what should happen over the next 90 days.

## Why This Matters

You are the first AI employee, not the entire AI organization.

The business now has a Chief of Staff and a Social and SEO Strategy Plan. The next question is which AI employees and systems should be built around that foundation.

## Use This Context

Use:

- Locked business overview
- Standing instructions
- AI org chart
- Context folder
- Mission 1 Social and SEO Strategy Plan
- Any current operations, email, CRM, customer service, content, or team-process materials available in the folder

## Definition Of Done

Create a folder named:

```text
02-ai-employee-roadmap/
```

Inside it, create:

1. **`roadmap-summary.md`**
   Include the executive summary, recommended sequence, assumptions, and 90-day path.

2. **`ai-org-chart.md`**
   Recommend the next AI employees. For each, include name or role, job description, manager, inputs needed, outputs produced, and why it matters now.

3. **`next-hires-priority.md`**
   Rank the next 3 to 5 AI employees by priority. Explain what to build now, what to defer, and what would create the most leverage.

4. **`systems-and-tools.md`**
   Recommend which systems, data sources, or tools each AI employee would need. Include content, SEO, email, CRM, communication, operations, and memory where relevant.

5. **`shared-memory-and-communication.md`**
   Explain how these AI employees should share context. Recommend the source of truth, folder structure, review cycle, and communication rhythm.

6. **`90-day-build-plan.md`**
   Propose a practical 90-day path with phases, milestones, and decision points.

## Roles To Consider

Consider whether the business needs:

- Content Producer
- SEO Researcher
- Video or Reels Producer
- Communications Officer
- CRM Analyst
- Operations Coordinator
- Memory Librarian
- Customer Success Reviewer
- Sales Follow-Up Assistant
- Reporting Analyst

Use different names if better names fit the business.

## Quality Bar

The roadmap should be:

- Specific to [BUSINESS NAME]
- Prioritized, not a wish list
- Honest about what should be built now versus later
- Clear about what data and access each AI employee needs
- Practical for the current team
- Useful enough that [OWNER NAME] can decide the next build step

## Guardrails

Do not:

- Recommend every possible AI employee at once
- Pretend disconnected chats are a real team
- Ignore shared memory and communication
- Recommend tools without explaining the operating reason
- Make this dependent on one vendor

The structure should be portable.

## How To Work

Use the AI org chart in `AGENTS.md`.

If useful, coordinate:

- Strategist for sequencing and tradeoffs
- Ops Coordinator for workflow and handoff design
- CRM Analyst for sales and customer data opportunities
- Memory Librarian for shared context and knowledge management
- Editor for clarity and realism

Bring [OWNER NAME] one complete draft for review.

## Clarify-Or-Go Instruction

If you need one or two answers that would materially change the roadmap, ask now.

Otherwise, make reasonable assumptions, label them, and proceed until you have a complete draft.
