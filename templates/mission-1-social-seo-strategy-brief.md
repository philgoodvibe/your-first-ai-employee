# Mission 1 - Social And SEO Strategy Plan

> **For [EMPLOYEE NAME]:** Read this brief in full before starting. Reference `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, the locked business overview, and everything useful in `context-folder/`.

## The Mission

Create a Social and SEO Strategy Plan for [BUSINESS NAME].

This plan should help [OWNER NAME] decide what the business should talk about, which formats fit the current team, which SEO themes are worth owning, and what the next 30 days of content should look like.

## Why This Matters

Content is the first visible test of whether you understand the business.

If you can study the business context and recommend a useful content strategy, you are not acting like a writing assistant. You are acting like a Chief of Staff preparing executive-level work for review.

## Use This Context

Use:

- `context-folder/00-business-overview.md`
- `context-folder/10-brand-voice/`
- `context-folder/20-products-services/`
- `context-folder/30-customers/`
- `context-folder/40-past-content/`
- Any sales pages, customer questions, testimonials, newsletters, call notes, or screenshots in the context folder

If context is thin, make reasonable assumptions, label them clearly, and tell [OWNER NAME] what additional material would improve the plan.

## Definition Of Done

Create a folder named:

```text
01-social-seo-strategy/
```

Inside it, create:

1. **`strategy-plan.md`**
   Include the executive summary, strategic recommendation, assumptions, risks, and next actions.

2. **`messaging-pillars.md`**
   Include 3 to 5 messaging pillars. For each pillar, explain why it matters, what the business should say, and what it should avoid saying.

3. **`content-formats.md`**
   Recommend the best content formats for the business. Include short-form video, carousel, newsletter, long-form article, podcast, webinar, or other formats only when they make sense.

4. **`seo-topic-clusters.md`**
   Recommend SEO topic clusters based on buyer intent, customer questions, and likely search behavior. Do not invent search volume. If you do not have data, say what should be researched next.

5. **`30-day-calendar.md`**
   Create a practical 30-day social media calendar. Include date or day number, platform, format, topic, hook, short description, and CTA.

6. **`recommended-ai-content-team.md`**
   Recommend the next AI employees that would help execute this strategy, such as Content Producer, SEO Researcher, Video Producer, Editor, or Analytics Reviewer.

## Quality Bar

The plan should be:

- Specific to [BUSINESS NAME]
- Realistic for the current team
- Clear enough for [OWNER NAME] to approve, correct, or reject
- Written in plain operator language
- Free of generic AI content ideas
- Connected to the business offer and customer problems

## Guardrails

Do not:

- Create generic content advice that could apply to any business
- Pretend you have SEO data you do not have
- Recommend a content volume the current team cannot sustain
- Ignore [OWNER NAME]'s voice
- Turn this into a full production system

Strategy comes first. Production comes later.

## How To Work

Use the AI org chart in `AGENTS.md`.

If useful, coordinate:

- Researcher for customer questions and SEO themes
- Strategist for messaging pillars and positioning
- Content Producer for calendar shape and format recommendations
- Editor for voice and generic-content review

Bring [OWNER NAME] one complete draft for review.

## Clarify-Or-Go Instruction

If you need one or two answers that would materially change the strategy, ask now.

Otherwise, make reasonable assumptions, label them, and proceed until you have a complete draft.
