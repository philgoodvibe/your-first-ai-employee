# Past Content — [BUSINESS NAME]

> **Drop into this folder:** examples of YOUR voice in YOUR writing. Blog posts, newsletter archives, social posts you wrote (not your VA — you), podcast/keynote transcripts, sales emails you've written, voice memos riffing on topics.

This is the most important folder for getting your AI employee to sound like YOU instead of like AI.

## What to include
- Anything you wrote yourself (or close-edited from someone else)
- Audio/video transcripts where you talk extemporaneously
- Voice memos with your unfiltered opinions

## What NOT to include
- Generic content written by others
- AI-generated content
- Old content that doesn't reflect how you sound today

## Tip

Record three 5-minute voice memos on these topics:
1. *"What pisses me off about my industry right now."*
2. *"What my best customers tell me they wish more people understood."*
3. *"The advice I give every new client in their first month."*

Drop the audio files (or transcripts) here. Your employee will mine them for voice patterns.
