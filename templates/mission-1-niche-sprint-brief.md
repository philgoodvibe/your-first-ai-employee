# Mission 1 — The 10x Niche Sprint

> **For [EMPLOYEE NAME]:** Read this brief in full before starting. Reference `context-folder/` throughout.

## The mission

Find the niche that, if [BUSINESS NAME] focused on it for the next 24 months, would 10x the business — and build the case for why.

## Why this matters

Most business owners spread themselves across too many niches and end up generic. Picking ONE niche to dominate compounds faster. Your job is to surface the best candidates and let [OWNER NAME] make the final call.

## Definition of done

A folder named `01-strategy/` in our working directory containing:

1. **`niche-options.md`** — 3 to 5 candidate niches. For each, include:
   - Niche name and TAM (rough estimate)
   - Why this niche has a 10x trajectory in the next 24 months (cite trends, search data, demand signals)
   - Why [BUSINESS NAME] is uniquely positioned to win in this niche
   - The biggest risk if we picked this niche
   - A confidence score 1–10 with reasoning

2. **`niche-recommendation.md`** — your top pick + a one-page argument for it. Include the case for, the case against, and the decision criteria [OWNER NAME] should use.

3. **`icp.md`** — for the recommended niche, the Ideal Client Profile:
   - Demographics (industry, role, company size, geography)
   - Psychographics (beliefs, fears, status concerns)
   - A day in their life (what they're doing when our marketing reaches them)
   - Where they hang out online (specific platforms, communities, influencers they follow)
   - What they're searching for (top 10 search queries)

4. **`pain-map.md`** — the top 7–10 pains this ICP has, ranked by intensity, with a verbatim quote from real online sources for each (Reddit, Quora, forums, podcast comments). Cite sources.

5. **`competitor-analysis.md`** — the top 5–10 players in this niche:
   - Who they are
   - How they position
   - What they do well
   - Where they fall short (cite reviews, complaints, gaps)
   - The opening for [BUSINESS NAME]

6. **`positioning-brief.md`** — a one-page brief specifying:
   - The single sentence positioning statement: "We help [WHO] [OUTCOME] without [FRICTION]."
   - The 3-word category we want to own
   - The angle that makes us different from every competitor
   - The single biggest objection and our response

## How to work

- Use sub-agents (see `AGENTS.md`). Spawn the **Researcher** for niche-options, ICP, pain-map, and competitor-analysis. Spawn the **Strategist** for the recommendation and positioning brief.
- Cite sources for every claim. We're going to make a real bet on this niche. The data has to be real.
- When you finish, leave a 5-bullet TL;DR at the top of `niche-recommendation.md` so [OWNER NAME] can review the conclusion in 60 seconds and dive into details if needed.

## When in doubt

Ask. Better to ask three clarifying questions than to ship a confident-but-wrong brief.
